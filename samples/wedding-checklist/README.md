# Wedding Checklist

Wedding Checklist helps you keep wedding tasks on track. Everything lives in one workbook.

## What's included
- The workbook: `tracker.xlsx`
- Demo rows: `example-data.csv`

## Setup
Keep `checklist.pdf` open while you work.
With the included sample rows, the Total Revenue cell shows 1,395 and the Total Cost cell shows 675.
