# Meal Prep Planner

Meal Prep Planner helps you plan a week of meals. Everything lives in one workbook.

## What's included
- The workbook: `tracker.xlsx`
- Demo rows: `example-data.csv`

## Setup
Read start-here.txt before you begin.
With the included sample rows, the Total Revenue cell shows 1,755 and the Total Cost cell shows 882.
