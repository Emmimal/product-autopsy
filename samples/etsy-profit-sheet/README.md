# Etsy Profit Sheet

Etsy Profit Sheet helps you see profit per listing. Everything lives in one workbook.

## What's included
- The workbook: `tracker.xlsx`
- Demo rows: `example-data.csv`

## Setup
Duplicate `config/settings.json` to change your currency.
With the included sample rows, the Total Revenue cell shows 1,537 and the Total Cost cell shows 634.
