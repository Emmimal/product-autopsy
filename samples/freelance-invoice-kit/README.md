# Freelance Invoice Kit

Freelance Invoice Kit helps you bill clients on time. Everything lives in one workbook.

## What's included
- The workbook: `tracker.xlsx`
- Demo rows: `example-data.csv`

## Setup
Copy `templates/invoice-template.docx` into your documents folder before you start.
With the included sample rows, the Total Revenue cell shows 1,388 and the Total Cost cell shows 815.
