# Study Planner

Study Planner helps you plan revision sessions. Everything lives in one workbook.

## What's included
- The workbook: `tracker.xlsx`
- Demo rows: `example-data.csv`

## Setup
Unzip `bonus-pack.zip` for extra templates.
Paste in the sample rows and revenue comes to 1,671 in total. With the included sample rows, the Total Cost cell shows 732.
