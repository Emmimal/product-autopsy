# Budget Planner

Budget Planner helps you track monthly spending. Everything lives in one workbook.

## What's included
- The workbook: `tracker.xlsx`
- Demo rows: `example-data.csv`

## Setup
Open `setup.xlsx` first and enter your starting balance.
With the included sample rows, the Total Revenue cell shows 1,581 and the Total Cost cell shows 752.
