# Habit Tracker

Habit Tracker helps you build daily habits. Everything lives in one workbook.

## What's included
- The workbook: `tracker.xlsx`
- Demo rows: `example-data.csv`

## Setup
Load `sample-clients.csv` to replace the demo rows with your own.
With the included sample rows, the Total Revenue cell shows 1,525 and the Total Cost cell shows 749.
