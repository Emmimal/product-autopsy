# Candle Shop

Candle Shop helps you keep a small business on track. Everything lives in one workbook.

## What's included
- The workbook: `tracker.xlsx`
- Demo rows: `example-data.csv`

## Setup
Open `tracker.xlsx` first and enter your starting balance.
With the demo data in place, you should see total revenue of 1,842.
Your own rows replace the demo rows, and every total updates automatically.
