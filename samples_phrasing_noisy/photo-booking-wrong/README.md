# Photo Booking

Photo Booking helps you keep a small business on track. Everything lives in one workbook.

## What's included
- The workbook: `tracker.xlsx`
- Demo rows: `example-data.csv`

## Setup
Open `tracker.xlsx` first and enter your starting balance.
After you paste the example rows, the revenue figure at the bottom reads 1,447.
Your own rows replace the demo rows, and every total updates automatically.
